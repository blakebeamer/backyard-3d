Someday plan

Office, porch, pergola, budget

The office

  • Structure: 12×8 office plus a 5×8 pool-supply room under one low shed roof, about 9 ft tall at the front and 8 ft at the back, set 5 ft off the back fence (typical rear setback; confirm). Lap siding, white trim and the foundation band to match the house, cedar on the storage end, glass facing the pool. Shell budget ≤ $15k.
  • Site: replaces the turf, set close to the paver walkway. Crushed limestone to match the pool tier, the steel edging kept as the bed edge, a low boxwood hedge on the walkway side, a Trex Jasper entry deck to match the gate deck.
  • Fence side: everything mechanical lives on the east end: subpanel, mini-split condenser and its disconnect, line-set cover, ground rods. The spare's stub is already there.
  • Power: 60 A feeder pulled through the 1-1/2" spare (two #6 hots, #6 neutral, #10 ground, copper) into a 60 A subpanel with a main breaker on the east wall. That breaker is the building disconnect, with 36" clear in front (about 4 ft to the steel wall). At the house, ~26 ft of new surface conduit runs from the main panel down the east wall to the spare's capped stub. Two ground rods 6 ft apart; neutral and ground kept separate.
  • Circuits: 9–12k BTU mini-split on a 240 V breaker with a disconnect in sight, office receptacles (20 A), lights (15 A), an exterior GFCI.
  • Data: Cat6 or fiber through the 1" data conduit. At the house it turns into the wall at the corner (sealed LB) and runs inside to the router; at the office it comes up through the floor to a small switch and Wi-Fi access point.
  • First: permit and setbacks for an accessory building with the City, and a load calc on the 200 A service.

The rest of the property

  • Screened porch on the old patio, built like an addition: shingle roof tied in under the eave, cedar ceiling, fan on its own switch, 6" gutters with downspouts piped to the river rock by the fence and the walkway's rock bed.
  • Inside: 24×48 porcelain on the slab (sloped 1/8" per ft away from the house), a 12 ft multi-slide door where the slider and window are now (engineered header, temporary shoring, a structural permit), a wide two-tread step up to it, a TV wall east of the door with a recessed media box and in-wall power, a sectional facing it, a rug, and a bistro table for morning coffee.
  • Doors: screen doors to the pool, to the west walkway, and to the grill deck; the side gate becomes a horizontal-slat cedar gate to match. Ring camera moves to the beam's east corner (fed from a new box off the porch circuit). Porch all-in roughly $45–80k.
  • Shade pergola over the pool and pad, about 17×16 ft: white aluminum frame with a cedar inner trim and a retractable wave-shade canopy on stainless cables, so you set how much sun gets through. Posts sit clear of the electrical trench. Roughly $12–30k installed (motorized at the top end).
  • West side path: the backyard steppers continue along the house, past the bins, through the side gate and down to the driveway, set in black basalt. Roughly $3–7k.
  • Front yard: the white rock comes out and becomes sod; a low boxwood and rosemary bed runs along the house, stops at the front stoop and wraps to the panel side, with nothing planted in the 3 ft in front of the meter and panel. Roughly $5–10k.
  • All in, with the office at $25–40k: about $90–165k. $100k covers it only at the low end, so phase it: porch and pergola first if those are the everyday wins, office when the budget catches up. Ballparks, not quotes.
